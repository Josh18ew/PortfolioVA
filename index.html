CREATIVE IDENTITY

My portfolio explores the development of my visual understanding and creative skills throughout the course in Reading Visual Arts. Each work reflects my growth in observing, analyzing, and applying the elements and principles of design. I experimented with different styles, from realistic observational drawings to abstract compositions, allowing me to express ideas in both structured and imaginative ways. Through activities like the transformation series and Filipino-inspired pattern design, I learned how culture, creativity, and technique can come together to create meaningful visual outcomes My process involved planning, sketching, and refining, which helped me appreciate the importance of patience and attention to detail. I also explored digital tools in creating posters, infographics, and meme series, expanding my skills beyond traditional media. Overall, this portfolio represents not only my finished artworks but also my learning journey, showing how I developed confidence in expressing ideas visually and understanding art more deeply.

Module Outputs

Module 1

This collage is a representation of myself and my digital visual world, which comprises social media, music, and digital tools that I use on a daily basis for both learning and entertainment.

Module 2

This piece is an observational drawing of everyday objects a water bottle, stapler, and a shoe applying the seven elements of art. It focuses on shape, form, and value through realistic shading, highlighting how light and shadow create a more lifelike and dimensional effect.

Module 3

This purely abstract artwork focuses on the principle of movement. I created a sense of motion by layering geometric and organic shapes so the viewer’s eyes naturally move diagonally across the piece.

Module 4

This transformation series shows how a single subject evolves across styles: representational (realistic and detailed), abstract (simplified but still recognizable), and non-objective (no clear subject, only lines, shapes, and colors)..

Module 5

This Filipino-inspired design features geometric patterns and bold colors, with my name in Baybayin at the center, highlighting cultural identity and balance.

Module 6–7

This pencil drawing of a glass and an apple focuses on realism through shading techniques like hatching, cross-hatching, and blending. It highlights depth, texture, and the use of light and shadow to create a three-dimensional effect.

Module 8

This digital poster promotes responsible AI use, highlighting fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability, and safety through clear visuals and a clean, engaging design.

Module 9

This three-photo story shows a typical day of an IT student from arriving on campus, studying in class, to going home highlighting growth, discipline, and balance.

Module 10

This infographic shows the most popular programming languages in 2026 using a clear layout, pie chart, and icons, with refined design for readability and balance.

Module 11

This meme shows how group work often becomes unfair when only one person does all the tasks. It uses humor to highlight the importance of responsibility, teamwork, and equal participation.
